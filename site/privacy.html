Privacy

Last updated 8 October 2026

  • Rundown runs on your computer. Your code, prompts and your agents' logs stay there. We never receive them.
  • The app sends us nothing about your work. If you say yes, it sends a small count of how much you used it, once a day.
  • This site keeps your email only if you ask for the release email, and only until you unsubscribe.
  • No ads, no tracking cookies, nothing sold.

The Rundown app and plugin

Rundown runs on your own computer. It reads the session logs that Claude Code and Codex write there, maps your project and keeps its database in Rundown's own folder on your machine. None of that is sent to us.

Usage stats, only if you say yes

The first time you use Rundown, it asks whether to share anonymous usage stats. Until you answer yes, it counts nothing and sends nothing. If you say yes, it sends one small report a day to this site:

WhatExample
A random id made on your computer, not derived from anything about you or it3f1c…
Rundown's version, your operating system and the map theme you use0.7.0, darwin, metro
Counts since the last report: times the app was opened, threads opened, replays played, live follows, threads shared, questions asked, projects opened, agent sessions and steps seenop 3, th 5
Your country, which the site reads from the request. Your IP address is not kept.SN

Never your code, file paths, prompts, thread titles or names. We use the reports to know how many people use Rundown, roughly where, and which parts matter. They are kept 13 months and then deleted automatically.

You can change your answer any time in Settings, or turn the stats off for good with DO_NOT_TRACK=1 in your environment. Settings also has Delete the stats already sent, which removes every report from your install. Our legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Asking Claude about your code

Ask is optional and needs your own Anthropic API key. When you ask a question, Rundown sends it to Anthropic with a short excerpt of the step and the code around it, after masking secrets. This goes from your computer to Anthropic under your key: Anthropic's terms and privacy policy apply, and we never see it.

Update check

Once a day the plugin downloads Rundown's latest version number from GitHub, to tell you when an update is out. The request carries nothing about you beyond what any web request does: GitHub sees your IP address.

Shared replays

Sharing saves a file on your computer. Secrets, email addresses, your home folder and your computer's name are masked first, and screenshots are never included. Where the file goes is up to you: we never receive it.

This website

Hosting

The site runs on Vercel, which processes technical data such as your IP address and browser to serve the pages and protect them from abuse. See Vercel's privacy policy.

Page statistics

We count page views with Vercel Web Analytics: the page, the site you came from, your country and the kind of device. It uses no cookies and doesn't follow you to other sites. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing how the site is used.

Fonts

The site serves its own fonts. Your browser doesn't contact a font service.

The release email

If you ask for the release email, we keep your email address, when you signed up and what you agreed to. We use it only to email you about Rundown's releases, when it's necessary. We send it with Resend, an email service. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing deletes your address. We keep it until you unsubscribe or we close the list. Our legal basis is your consent.

Where the data is kept

Release emails and usage reports are kept in private storage at Vercel, in the United States. Vercel processes them for us under its Data Processing Addendum, which includes the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers outside the European Economic Area. Only we can read them.

When we send the release email, your address goes to Resend, which delivers it from the United States. Resend processes it for us under its Data Processing Addendum, which includes the Standard Contractual Clauses, and is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we have about you, correct it, delete it, receive a copy, or object to how we use it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Email sydjbrl@gmail.com. For usage stats, send your install id, shown in Settings, or use Delete the stats already sent. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: the CNIL in France, where Rundown is based, or the authority of the country where you live.

Children

Rundown is a tool for developers and isn't meant for children under 16. We don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child gave us their email, write to us and we'll delete it.
