## Recording copy

Three agents. Building at once. Files changing everywhere. Do you know what changed?

Meet Brainstorm. A live map of your code, and the agents writing it. Keep working in Claude Code. Open Brainstorm to see the work take shape.

Follow a session. See each action, with a short, readable label. Open an edit to see exactly what changed.

And when something needs explaining, ask. Why this change? What does it affect? Get an answer grounded in the step you're looking at.

Zoom out to the whole project. See how files connect, where agents are working, and what each part does.

NVIDIA Nemotron runs on our Brev GPU, turning code into summaries and agent actions into readable labels.

We built Brainstorm with agents. Now we can follow their work, and understand what they're building.

Let the agents type. Keep your brain in the loop.

## Editorial decisions

- This draft moves the research statistics out of the voice-over to give the real product room. Research can live in the submission card, with verified sources.
- No claims about pause/steer, private mode, history, or measured savings until those are demonstrated.
- At capture time, replace concept UI with real footage. Show only shipped features. If live activity is not working, change the matching sentence and shot together.
- “Built with itself all day” is deliberately absent: the current build log does not support it.
- Narration is 143 words; record calmly with pauses. Final timings follow the actual voice recording.
