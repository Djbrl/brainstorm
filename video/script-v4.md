# Brainstorm — 90s script v4 (problem → app → future)

Film: `src/film-v4.tsx` → `out/film-v4.mp4`. Same visuals as v3 for 0–65s; benchmark shortened to 65–72s; new **Future** scene 72–83s replaces "Read once".

## Recording copy (~150 words)

**Problem (0–31s)**
Brainstorm. A live map of your code, and the agents writing it.
One agent, you can follow. Three, and the changes scatter across your project.
A wall of edits tells you what changed. Nobody reads it.
In one study, developers using AI scored lower on comprehension. Those who asked for explanations understood more.

**The app (31–72s)**
So follow a session. Open an edit. Ask why. Then zoom out to see where agents are working.
Local listeners read session logs, a mapper follows imports, SQLite keeps the record.
Nemotron on our Brev GPU summarizes the code. Claude answers your questions.
Three hundred eleven files in seventy-three seconds, about two cents of GPU time.

**The future (72–90s)**
Next: every coding agent, not just one. Privacy by project. Summaries measured for accuracy. And shared maps for whole teams.
Let the agents type. Keep your brain in the loop.
